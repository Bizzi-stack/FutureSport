Barbados Schools Football League (BSFL)
Website & Management System Development Requirements
1. Project Overview
Objective
Develop a modern league management platform to manage all BSFL competitions, registrations, fixtures, results, statistics and website content. The initial objective is to deliver a stable Version 1 (MVP) for live testing before the end of August.
The platform should support all current and future BSFL competitions while maintaining historical records for schools, players and competitions.
